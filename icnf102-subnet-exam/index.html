Subnetting long questions

Twenty questions written like the long questions in the practice papers, from the exact past-paper question up to VLSM. Work each one on paper first, type your answers in to have them marked, then open the memo to see the working one step at a time.

1. Work it on paperWrite every step: binary mask, bits borrowed, new mask, increment, hosts.
2. Type and checkMasks can be typed as 255.255.255.192 or /26. Ticks and crosses come back in red pen.
3. Read the memoReveal the next step to get unstuck without seeing the whole answer. Your work is saved in this browser.
