How to use this page

1. Work it on paper
Write every step: the binary mask, bits borrowed, new mask, increment and hosts.
2. Type and check
Fill in the boxes and press Check my answers. Each box gets a tick or a cross and you get a mark.
3. Read the memo
Show next step reveals the working one line at a time, so you can get unstuck without seeing the whole answer.
