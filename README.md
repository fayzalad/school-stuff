# school-stuff

Study material, one module per folder. Open the [home page](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/) to pick a module; each module has its own page that sorts its guides, quizzes, drills and exam practice. To add a module or page, edit the `MODULES` list in `shared/site.js` (a new module also needs a copy of a `<slug>/index.html` folder).

## Modules

### ICNF102 — Communication Network Fundamentals
- **[Field reference](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/icnf102-study-guide/)** — 21-sheet study guide covering the whole module: communication rules, OSI/TCP/IP models, physical and data link layers, IP addressing, subnetting and VLSM, transport and application layers, DHCP, NAT, VLANs, routing and RIPv2, plus a reference appendix and a case-study walkthrough. Explanations only, no questions.
- **[Line Test](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/icnf102-quiz/)** — 200 multiple-choice questions in 8 sets of 25, rising in difficulty. Study mode explains each answer as you go; Test mode holds feedback until the end. Every question is explained, so it doubles as revision. Every question also has an on-demand hint.
- **[Subnet Bench](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/icnf102-subnetting/)** — traditional subnetting practice: given a network and a required number of subnets, work through bits borrowed, new mask, hosts per subnet, and the first two subnets by hand, with the method explained at every step. Generates a new problem each time.

### WRAV102 / MSEV102 — Semester Test 2 theory
- **[Theory Drill](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/wrav102-drill/)** — 100 multiple-choice questions covering the Section A theory topics: sorting algorithms (bubble, selection, insertion), searching and sorted state (linear vs binary), classes/list classes/arraylists/arrays/text files/position rules, and code tracing and applied questions. Answers save to your browser. Four tabs, 25 questions each.

### STAV102 — Business Statistics, final exam (Units 1–10)
- **[Exam Study Guide](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/stav102-exam-guide/)** — all ten units: the Test 1 and Test 2 guide sections plus new Units 9 (regression and correlation) and 10 (time series), the exam layout and what past finals ask, reading MS Excel output, errata, traps, exam technique and a 2-day plan.
- **[Past-Paper Questions](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/stav102-past-papers/)** — every MCQ and long question from 8 past STAV102 tests and finals (typed out, repeats merged, most-asked first). MCQs show the working; long questions are taught step by step with a practise-again list.
- **[Exam Quiz](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/stav102-exam-quiz/)** — 275 multiple-choice questions across Units 1–10 with worked explanations; filter by unit, a 50-question exam sim in the paper's unit mix, and a redo-wrong-answers mode.

### STAV102 — Business Statistics, Semester Test 2 (Units 5–8)
- **[Study Guide](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/stav102-study-guide/)** — probability distributions (discrete, binomial, Poisson, normal, inverse normal), sampling distributions and the CLT, confidence intervals (μ, p, σ², sample size) and hypothesis testing (one- and two-population, paired), with worked examples from the notes, example packs and unit memos, a "which test?" decision tree, what the lecture videos add, an errata table for the notes and memos, exam traps and a 7-day plan.
- **[MCQ Drill](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/stav102-quiz/)** — 68 multiple-choice questions in the test's a)–e) style with a worked explanation for every answer. Filter by unit; "test sim" serves 20 random questions; wrong answers are remembered in your browser so you can redo only those.
- **[Explained MCQs](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/stav102-explained-mcq/)** — 50 original multiple-choice questions for Units 5–8 with every answer explained (why it is right, and the trap to avoid). Built for skimming; a "hide answers" tick lets you test yourself first.
- **[Mock Papers](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/stav102-mock-papers/)** — two full mock tests in the real layout (Part 1 long questions [30] + Part 2 MCQ [20], 90 minutes) with mark-by-mark memos hidden until opened.
- **[Cheat Sheet](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/stav102-cheat-sheet/)** — one printable page of the decisions that aren't on the formula sheet: which distribution, which interval, which test, critical values, rejection rules and conclusion templates.

### IAUT202 — Introduction to Automation
Organised by exam part (5 questions, 95 marks).

**1. Theory and robotics**
- **[Question 1 Quiz](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/iaut202-quiz/)** — 100 easy multiple-choice questions on the short-answer theory topics, with quiz and memorise modes.
- **[Likely Question 1 (50)](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/iaut202-likely-q1/)** — 50 questions on the Question 1 topics most likely to come up.
- **[ADC and Number Systems Quiz](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/iaut202-adc-quiz/)** — 26 questions for Question 2: computed successive approximation conversions, resolution, quantisation error and number conversions.
- **[Robotics Quiz (79)](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/iaut202-robotics-quiz/)** — Chapter 8: joint types L, O, R, T and V, the five robot configurations, wrist and drives, sensors, control types, end effectors, programming, and accuracy and repeatability.

**2. PLC**
- **[PLC Quiz](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/iaut202-plc-quiz/)** — 38 questions: scan cycle, contacts, latches, timers, counters, flashers and scenario design.
- **[PLC Scenario Practice (6)](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/iaut202-plc-scenarios/)** — six packaging and conveyor scenarios in the style of Question 3, each with an I/O table and full logic memo.

**3. Pneumatics**
- **[Pneumatics Quiz](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/iaut202-pneumatics-quiz/)** — 34 questions: cylinders, valves, ports, sequence notation, the cascade method and drawing conventions.
- **[Pneumatic Cascade Practice (9)](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/iaut202-pneumatics-practice/)** — nine sequences to group and turn into an electrical cascade, with memos (groups, relays, solenoids).

**4. Mock exams**
- **[Mock Exam Papers A, B and C](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/iaut202-practice-paper/)** — three full 95-mark papers in the exam layout and the make-up paper's style (20 multiple-choice theory questions, 5-bit ADC, PLC scenario with I/O table and sequence of operation, robotics with pictures, pneumatic cascade with lamp), each question with a memo that opens on tap.
- **[Mixed Mock Quiz (64)](https://fayzalad.github.io/school-stuff/iaut202-mock-quiz/)** — a mixed set sampled from every exam part, filterable by part.
