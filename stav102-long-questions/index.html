How the answers are rounded (same as the memos)

  • z is rounded to 2 decimals before you use the table; table probabilities have 4 decimals.
  • Critical values come from the 2026 formula sheet: z 1.645 / 1.96 / 2.326 / 2.576, the t-table (3 dp) and the χ² table (2 dp).
  • Answers within a small rounding margin are marked right, so 2.57 vs 2.576 won't cost you a tick here (but use the sheet's value in the exam).
  • Sample proportions use the normal approximation without a continuity correction, as Funda and the memos do.
